## V136 — V127 3-model Bayesian-weighted ensemble

### Experiment
- 3 모델: pair_wide, pair_deep, trans_wide
- 20 seeds × GroupKFold 5-fold × Isotonic calibration + mean matching
- Per-target Bayesian weight optimization (30 restarts)
- Comparison: Bayesian optimized vs V127 fixed (0.35/0.25/0.40) vs equal weight

### Results

| Target | V127 LL | Opt LL | Equal LL | Δ(opt-v127) |
|--------|---------|--------|----------|-------------|
| Q1     | 0.55840 | 0.55112| 0.55235  | **+0.00729** |
| Q2     | 0.54433 | 0.54665| 0.54169  | -0.00232 |
| Q3     | 0.56039 | 0.56294| 0.56168  | -0.00255 |
| S1     | 0.52171 | 0.52360| 0.51991  | -0.00189 |
| S2     | 0.53014 | 0.53725| 0.53199  | -0.00711 |
| S3     | 0.51830 | 0.51499| 0.51363  | **+0.00331** |
| S4     | 0.58135 | 0.58025| 0.58066  | +0.00110 |
| **AVG**| 0.54495 | 0.54526| 0.54313  | -0.00031 |

### Key Findings

1. **Bayesian optimization barely helps on average**: Δ=-0.00031 (negligible)
2. **Equal weight beats Bayesian** on average (0.54313 vs 0.54526)
3. **V127 fixed weights in the middle** (0.54495) — not the best, not the worst
4. **Per-target, Bayesian helps Q1 (+0.007) and S3 (+0.003)** but hurts Q2, Q3, S1, S2
5. **pair_deep dominates** in Bayesian weights for Q1 (0.74), S3 (0.88), S4 (0.78)
6. **pair_wide dominates** for Q2 (0.68), S1 (0.61)

### Substantive insight from V256 (reconfirmed)
- **pair_wide** is the most stable single model (V256: top-2 in 5/7 targets)
- **pair_deep** is strongest for S-series (S1, S3, S4)
- **trans_wide** is weak individually but adds diversity for Q-series
- **base models (V115) are weak alone** — V256 confirmed they add almost nothing
- **V128 (LOO target encoding) was disastrously worse** (+0.11 OOF)
